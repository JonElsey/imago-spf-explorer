SPF data

The SPF (Sun Probability Framework) cloud probability data was generated by Imago, using Sentinel-2 satellite imagery. An example of this data can be found here. More information about this data can be found in the Metadata and Lineage sections of that page.

Area boundaries

Area boundaries are obtained from the Imago LSOA boundaries dataset. This data is derived from data from the Office for National Statistics in England and Wales, National Records of Scotland in Scotland, and the Northern Ireland Statistics and Research Agency in Northern Ireland. The data is licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. For more information see the Lineage section of the LSOA boundaries dataset page.
