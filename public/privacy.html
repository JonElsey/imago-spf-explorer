Privacy

This page explains what information the SPF Explorer needs to run, where it is sent and why it is needed.

Who is responsible

[Organisation name] is the data controller for this website. You can contact our data protection officer at [email address].

When you visit

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses. For more information see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Local storage. The site uses your browser's local storage to store preferences, such as whether you have dismissed the help panel. This information is stored only on your device and is not used or sent anywhere else.

Background map. Map information is provided by OpenFreeMap. OpenFreeMap state that they do not typically store IP addresses, but they may be logged for security purposes or if errors occur. For more information see the OpenFreeMap Privacy Policy.

Visitor statistics. We use Umami to count visits to the site. It records the page visited, the referring website, your browser, operating system, device type and country. It does not use cookies and does not store your IP address.

When you search for a place

What you type into the place search is sent to Nominatim, a geocoding service run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation, along with your IP address. This allows the explorer to find the location that you are searching for. See the Nominatim privacy policy for more information.

When you use your location

Your browser will ask for permission to share your location. Your exact location is used inside your browser to find nearby sunny areas and is not stored by Imago. An approximate (rounded to 1 kilometre) location is sent to Open-Meteo to get live weather, as described below.

Live weather

When you use "Find me a sunny place", the location you searched for or your own location is rounded to about 1 km and sent to Open-Meteo, along with the locations of the nearby sunny areas and your IP address. This is used to get the current cloud cover at each point. Open-Meteo's logs can contain these coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. See the Open-Meteo privacy policy for more information.

Where your information goes

Some of the services above, including GitHub, are based outside the UK, so your information may be processed outside the UK.

Your rights

You have rights under data protection law, including the right to access your personal data, the right to rectification, the right to erasure, the right to restrict processing and the right to object to processing. If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact us using the details provided above.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
