Privacy

The SPF Explorer doesn't ask for your name or any other personal details, and doesn't use cookies.

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical information such as your IP address.
  • Background map. Map information is provided by OpenFreeMap, which may log IP addresses for security purposes or if errors occur.
  • Visitor statistics. We count visits using Umami. It does not use cookies and does not store your IP address.
  • Local storage. Your browser remembers whether you have dismissed the help panel. This is stored only on your device.
  • When you search for a place. What you type is sent to Nominatim, a geocoding service run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation, along with your IP address.
  • When you use your location. Your browser asks for permission first. Your exact location is used only inside your browser and is not stored by Imago.
  • Live weather. "Find me a sunny place" sends a location rounded to about 1 km to Open-Meteo, along with your IP address. Open-Meteo deletes its logs after 90 days.

Imago is run by the University of Liverpool. For full details, including how to contact us and your rights, see the Imago privacy notice.
